# Original Source Prompts

以下为用户提供的三份中英文原始 Prompt，作为 Skill 的设计来源与回溯样例。

## 1. 羊毛毡草莓

🧶 **中文版本**
主题：
一只手握着一把由羊毛毡制成的微型软刀，正在切割一颗微缩的羊毛毡草莓。草莓只有拇指大小，由红色羊毛毡手工制作而成，表面毛茸茸、质感细腻，能看到细小的羊毛纤维和针扎的纹理。切开的草莓内部同样由羊毛毡组成，果肉结构清晰，颜色略浅，呈现出柔软蓬松的层次感，展现浓厚的手工质感。

声音风格：
ASMR风格，柔和的羊毛摩擦声，轻盈、干净，带有治愈感和温暖的空气感。

视觉效果：
超清4K特写，镜头固定。焦点集中在羊毛毡刀与微缩草莓的接触点，展示羊毛的纤维细节。羊毛毡刀外观柔软蓬松，但边缘整齐。背景是中性色调的布艺桌面或木质砧板，衬托出羊毛的柔和纹理。

灯光与镜头：
柔和的自然窗光，形成温暖的高光与柔和阴影。浅景深让微缩场景更具真实感与立体感。

动作：
羊毛毡刀干净利落地切下，多次切割。每一次切割都平稳而轻柔，刀锋穿过柔软的羊毛纤维。伴随每一次切割，被切开的微缩羊毛毡草莓片轻轻倒在砧板上，依然保持毛茸茸的立体质感。整个过程节奏柔和、整洁且极具疗愈感。

✨ **English Version**
Theme:
A hand holds a miniature soft knife made of felted wool, slicing a tiny felted wool strawberry. The strawberry is about the size of a thumb, handcrafted from red wool felt. Its surface appears fluffy and delicate, showing fine wool fibers and subtle needle-felting textures. The inside of the strawberry is also made of wool felt, with clearly defined fruit structure in a slightly lighter shade of red. The interior appears soft and airy, with gentle layers that highlight a strong handmade aesthetic.

Sound Style:
ASMR-inspired — soft wool friction sounds that are light, clean, and soothing, evoking a warm and comforting atmosphere.

Visual Style:
Ultra-clear 4K macro close-up with a fixed camera. The focus is on the contact point between the felted wool knife and the miniature strawberry, showcasing the fiber details of the wool. The felt knife looks soft and fluffy but has a clean, even edge. The background features a neutral-toned fabric surface or a wooden cutting board that complements the gentle wool texture.

Lighting & Camera:
Soft natural window light creates warm highlights and gentle shadows. The shallow depth of field enhances the realism and three-dimensionality of the miniature scene.

Action:
The felted wool knife cuts smoothly and cleanly through the strawberry, with multiple gentle slicing motions. Each cut is steady and light, as the blade passes through the soft wool fibers. With every slice, the tiny felted strawberry segments gently fall onto the cutting board, retaining their fluffy, three-dimensional texture. The entire process feels calm, tidy, and deeply soothing.
